CRYPTO COMPLIANCE

Crypto AML & AI automation

Overview

An AML case can combine ledger entries, wallet labels and AI-generated prose. Treat them as different kinds of evidence. Reconcile amounts and times with deterministic tools, and preserve the source and uncertainty of any inferred wallet connection.

Use an approved environment for drafting. A reviewer must be able to trace each material claim to a record, correct unsupported language and reconstruct who authorized the final decision.

Partner handoff

A blockchain analytics provider can explain a label’s source and confidence. The investigator corroborates it with transaction records. A designated liaison handles any authorized referral, and a human reviewer signs the case decision.

How can AI speed crypto AML case preparation while every material claim remains traceable to evidence?

Separate calculation, data routing and narrative drafting. Use deterministic tools for amounts and times, an approved model for evidence-linked drafts and an authorized reviewer for contrary evidence and reporting decisions.

Decision trade-off

An external model may reduce drafting time while client data, wallet labels and STR drafts carry confidentiality risks. Internal models still need access, log-retention and inference controls.

Research themes

What may an AI agent execute?

Authorize reading, drafting, proposing transactions and moving money separately. If a tool can move funds, specify amount and counterparty limits, independent approvals, a traceable action history and a way to suspend execution.

Tracing an AI-drafted STR to evidence

Populate times, amounts and parties from verified fields. A reviewer should trace material statements to evidence, flag unresolved inferences and record reasons for edits. The authorized person makes the filing decision.

Personal devices and unapproved AI

Use a training scenario to trace which services receive case text after it leaves the case system, who can access it and how long it persists. Provide approved tools and a route for questions, then test staff with a comparable sensitive-data scenario.

Handing off a 24-hour payment incident

On-chain confirmation, bank payout and human review can keep different hours. An overnight handoff should identify the asset’s last known location, remaining interventions, authorized contacts and time last checked. The incoming team verifies the position before taking ownership.

From risk to control and evidence

Combine on-chain evidence, fiat records and human review across a 24/7 market. Preserve uncertainty in address attribution and keep protected data within approved processing routes.

How to measure effectiveness

Share of sampled material claims traceable to a dated source and the level of confidence it supports. Review out-of-hours cases as a separate sample.

Case analysis

Illustrative scenario

A provider flags indirect exposure at an external wallet. The analyst checks hashes, label dates and provenance, corrects a draft that changed “possible link” into “confirmed illicit,” and preserves the edit trail.

Official references

  • OSFI

    Guideline E-23 · Model Risk Management (2027)

    The revised guideline takes effect on 1 May 2027 for federally regulated financial institutions within its scope. It ties how models are reviewed, approved and monitored to their inherent risk.

    Open official source
  • FINTRAC

    Financial transaction reporting thresholds and deadlines

    FINTRAC lists the triggers and deadlines by report type. For completed or attempted transactions, a suspicious transaction report is due as soon as practicable once there are reasonable grounds to suspect money laundering, terrorist financing or sanctions evasion.

    Open official source
  • FINTRAC

    Private-to-private information sharing

    Reporting entities can share personal information under an approved code of practice. The guidance sets out who can participate and what conditions apply to each disclosure.

    Open official source
