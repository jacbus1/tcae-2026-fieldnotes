FINTRAC examinations

Overview

An examination sample should reveal what staff knew, which procedure was in force and what happened when the control encountered an exception. Rebuild the decision from records created at the time, including source transactions, rule versions and staff actions.

Where evidence reveals a failure, establish the affected population before declaring the issue closed. Independent retesting should cover both previously missed items and a later period of ordinary operation.

Partner handoff

An advisor can test whether the evidence supports management’s response. The designated compliance contact submits dated facts to the examiner, tracks outstanding requests and assigns each remediation item to an internal owner.

If an examiner samples an exception, can the institution reconstruct what happened and test the subsequent fix?

A control needs evidence of suitable design, operation during the sample period and effectiveness against its risk. Pair staff walkthroughs with system records to expose gaps in the documented flow.

Decision trade-off

A demonstration explains design. Samples across periods, channels and exceptions show daily operation. Define the population before judging what the sample leaves uncovered.

Research themes

A requested transaction falls through

When a customer withdraws a request or an institution declines it, record the steps already taken, amount and reason it stopped. Preserve facts that support and weaken suspicion, along with when enough information existed to assess the reporting threshold.

Reconstructing the control in effect

Retrieve the policy and rule versions in force when the case arose. Trace the operator, exceptions, reviews and outcome. If practice and policy diverged, describe the discrepancy, period affected and population exposed.

Responding to a finding or notice

For each finding, identify agreed points, specific disputed facts, evidence and completed remediation. On receipt of a formal notice, check the response route, deadline and representation requirements against the notice and applicable law. Track formal proceedings and remediation separately.

Filing deadlines and retention periods

For multiple cash or virtual-currency receipts, reconstruct receipt times and the relationships relevant to aggregation. Determine record retention separately using the sector, record type and event that starts the retention period.

From risk to control and evidence

Test whether staff can reconstruct a sampled decision using the procedure, data and rule version available on that day. Include exceptions and a later period after any repair.

How to measure effectiveness

Share of sampled decisions a reviewer can reconstruct from contemporaneous evidence, reported separately for routine cases and exceptions.

Case analysis

Illustrative scenario

A new payment rail only partly reaches monitoring. Reconcile source and received counts and amounts, establish the affected period, backfill and replay, then independently sample both the replay and subsequent normal runs.

Official references

    Assessment manual for compliance examinations

    The manual follows an examination from scoping to findings. It explains how examiners use interviews, policies and transaction samples to test whether controls operate in practice.

  • OSFI

    How OSFI and FINTRAC work together

    OSFI supervises the financial soundness of federally regulated institutions, and FINTRAC oversees anti-money laundering and anti-terrorist financing compliance. The page explains how the agencies share relevant supervisory information.

    Open official source
  • FINTRAC

    Financial transaction reporting thresholds and deadlines

    FINTRAC lists the triggers and deadlines by report type. For completed or attempted transactions, a suspicious transaction report is due as soon as practicable once there are reasonable grounds to suspect money laundering, terrorist financing or sanctions evasion.
