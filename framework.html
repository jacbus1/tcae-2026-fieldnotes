FRAMEWORK

The compliance framework

Overview

Start with one service and one decision. Follow the activity from the customer to the team that can act, identify where it could fail, and choose a control with a named owner. Use technology to check the evidence and a person with delegated authority to decide what happens next.

Test the result in the conditions staff actually face. A missed weekend payout or a growing queue of high-risk alerts reveals how incentives, operational dependencies and control ownership interact.

How corporate culture shapes the decision

Start with a decision that put a service target or a commercial goal under pressure. Trace what staff raised, how management responded and whether the customer or control outcome changed.

Four cross-topic comparisons

Accountability and data diverge

Stablecoin partners hold separate issuance, custody and fiat records. Gaming parties may likewise see only wagers or payments.

How to test it
Request the same transaction from the accountable entity and the data holder. Compare gaps and retrieval times.

Case closure and victim outcomes

A report, warning, intervention and recovery are distinct events. One victim can generate several records.

How to test it
Deduplicate by case, event and victim. Track missing outcomes before calculating rates.

Finding a gap and proving repair

Examiners need sampled evidence. Boards must decide what residual risk to accept. A remediation file should support both.

How to test it
Reperform an affected sample, check for repeat failures and link results to the funding decision and review date.

Worked example · a weekend payout delay

LensIllustrative stablecoin payment
What happenedOn Saturday, a sender sees the token transfer settle. The recipient still cannot use the local currency.
Who decidesOperations checks payout timing against the service promise. An investigator assesses suspicious activity. Customer support explains the delay and available remedies.
What proves the outcomeMatch the transaction hash, fiat settlement reference and time the recipient could access the funds. If delays recur, record the corrective decision and retest on a later weekend.
