Overview

The 2026 conference examined how a financial institution turns a warning into action. Canada’s mutual evaluation and FINTRAC supervision framed the morning discussion. Stablecoins, AI, casino activity and fraud disruption showed where a customer or an investigation can be lost between teams.

The fieldnotes apply one framework across eight topics: understand the business, identify the risks, design the controls, leverage technology, and maintain human accountability. Each topic traces a decision to the person who can act and the evidence that shows the outcome.
