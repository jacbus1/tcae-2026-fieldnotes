Regulators

TopicQuestionEvidenceDecision
Stablecoins & tokenizationWhich entity receives funds, issues tokens or performs a payment function?Entity chart, product terms, funds flow and activity by jurisdiction.Assess scope by activity and identify questions requiring clarification.
Monitoring & financial intelligenceCan a reviewer reconstruct the grounds for suspicion from the transaction facts?Narrative, chronology, material transactions, sources and disposition.Review reporting quality and whether decisions have a consistent evidential basis.
AI governance & RegTechHow does the institution know an update preserves control over the original risk?Version comparisons, sample results, change approval and scope of reliance.Examine whether the use stays within demonstrated capability.
FINTRAC examinationsWere exceptions in the same period detected and addressed?Sampling population, design, operating records and exception handling.Examine evidence of an isolated error or systemic weakness and its reach.
Fraud disruption & law enforcementAre the purpose, authority and limits of sharing and intervention documented?Legal basis, access log, minimum disclosure and reporting decision.Review permitted sharing and suspicious-transaction handling separately.
Boards & risk governanceWhen did the board learn of the issue and how did it track remediation?Escalation chronology, minutes, issue register and retest evidence.Assess whether oversight and challenge translated into follow-through.
Gaming & casino financial crimeCan the supervised entity obtain the payment and betting records its controls require?Local authorization, operating agreement, data handoff inventory, exceptions and report samples.Examine statutory responsibility, operational control and data gaps.
Crypto AML & AI automationCan the final narrative be traced to transactions and judgments available at the time?Source snapshots, transaction evidence IDs, human edits and approval records.Review reporting basis, completeness and timeliness.

Boards & executives

TopicQuestionEvidenceDecision
Stablecoins & tokenizationHow long can liquidity and staffing sustain a weekend redemption surge?Stress scenarios, available reserves, custody concentration and open exceptions.Decide launch conditions against limits, contingencies and unresolved exposure.
Monitoring & financial intelligenceAs throughput improves, are material links and residual exposure still being identified?Age of high-risk cases, independent QA, cross-case referrals and verified fixes.Resource complex investigations and set risk tolerance without rewarding individual filing volumes.
AI governance & RegTechDo multiple critical controls depend on one source or provider?Dependency map, material incidents, fallback tests and exit costs.Set a concentration tolerance and fund contingencies and deadlines.
FINTRAC examinationsWhat exposure remains and when can remediation be verified?Exposure window, milestones, interim controls and independent retest report.Fund action, set review dates and require residual-risk reporting if delayed.
Fraud disruption & law enforcementWhat harm was observably reduced, and how confidently can outcomes be attributed?Outcome definitions, amount categories, observation windows, false stops and complaints.Evaluate investment using intervention time and client outcomes, documenting uncertainty.
Boards & risk governanceWhat risk remains under each option and what does a quarter of delay imply?Metrics with baselines and denominators, material assumptions, options and review dates.Approve resources within mandate or request revisions, recording the basis.
Gaming & casino financial crimeWhat risks become unobservable if an operator or platform withholds data?Data dependencies, uncovered channels, compensating controls, complaints and remediation.Require data access, restrict exposure or revisit contracts and review arrangements.
Crypto AML & AI automationIs time saved accompanied by more attribution errors or privacy incidents?QA by use case, material error rates, review effort and exception incidents.Set pilot scope, expansion criteria and stop conditions.

Business & operations

TopicQuestionEvidenceDecision
Stablecoins & tokenizationWhich leg has actually settled when the customer sees “completed”?On-chain confirmation, ledger posting, fiat credit and redemption hours.Align status messages and handoffs, assigning an owner to unsettled legs.
Monitoring & financial intelligenceWhich transaction fields are lost or delayed before monitoring?Source-to-monitoring reconciliations, field definitions and latency logs.Repair the feed and determine the backfill and replay window.
AI governance & RegTechWhich services can continue if scoring stops or becomes unreliable?Workflow dependencies, operating hours, manual capacity and rehearsed fallback.Restrict a use, switch processing or suspend reliance.
FINTRAC examinationsWhat do operators actually do when an exception falls outside the procedure?Shift logs, exception tickets, sign-offs and handoff timestamps.Update procedures so each exception has a recipient and due date.
Fraud disruption & law enforcementCan the payment still be paused or recalled, and who authorizes it?Payment status, rail deadlines, authority policy and receiving-party acknowledgement.Take authorized action within the intervention window and set a review time.
Boards & risk governanceWhich team and shift inherit the workload from an interim control?Transaction windows, exception volumes, capacity and client waiting times.Commit to sustainable coverage, handoffs and exit criteria for temporary measures.
Gaming & casino financial crimeCan each player transaction be joined to betting and promotions?Player identifiers, payment and wagering logs, promotion terms and rail reconciliation.Repair missing joins and exception handoffs, assigning owners in procedures.
Crypto AML & AI automationWho takes a night alert or case during a model outage?Duty roster, triage rules, manual-takeover tests and handoff acknowledgement.Reserve human coverage and escalation times for priority work.

AML investigators

TopicQuestionEvidenceDecision
Stablecoins & tokenizationHow can source of funds, wallet movement and beneficiary be connected?Client context, transfer references, transaction hashes and confidence in address attribution.Separate verified and uncertain links before deciding on EDD, escalation or reporting review.
Monitoring & financial intelligenceCould the shared counterparty be a common service provider?KYC, counterparty role, time relationships, on-chain confidence labels and counterevidence.Document uncertainty and choose closure, complex-case referral or STR assessment.
AI governance & RegTechCan I explain a historical score, verify its inputs and preserve contrary evidence?Timestamps, model version, source evidence, confidence and review trail.Decide the case on independent evidence and refer recurring errors to the model owner.
FINTRAC examinationsCan the decision be reviewed using only information available at the time?Original alert, rule in force, case versions, timestamps and review notes.Reconstruct the original decision and record subsequent facts and reopening criteria separately.
Fraud disruption & law enforcementHow can a victim be protected while preserving facts for later investigation?Transaction chronology, independent callback result, original signal and action rationale.Document protection, STR assessment and external referral as separate decisions.
Boards & risk governanceWhich priority cases are constrained by data or capacity?Age by risk tier, QA defects and anonymized case illustrations.Recommend an evidence-led priority and identify remaining information needs.
Gaming & casino financial crimeCould play, promotions or harm explain the anomaly, and what contradicts each account?Wagering and cash-out patterns, source of funds, behaviour, staff observations and counterevidence.Document the grounds for AML, fraud and responsible-gambling referrals separately.
Crypto AML & AI automationIs each link an observed fact, provider label or inference?Hashes, values, source and timestamps, confidence and alternative explanations.Own the case and filing decisions and assess any restriction under separate authority.

Customers & victims

TopicQuestionEvidenceDecision
Stablecoins & tokenizationWho accepts my redemption, and where can I find timing, fees and recourse?Applicable terms, fee disclosure, receipt and reachable service provider.Choose a route on its terms and seek a trace from the named contact if overdue.
Monitoring & financial intelligenceWhat information can clarify a legitimate transaction?Purpose, counterparty relationship, supporting records and prior communications.Submit clarifying evidence through an approved channel and follow up on an unresolved service issue.
AI governance & RegTechHow can I correct records or seek review after a mistaken match?Permitted service explanation, clarifying identification and review channel.Request correction or human review and have the institution track resolution.
Fraud disruption & law enforcementHow can I verify the caller safely and track my funds?Trusted channel, payment reference, provider responses and incident reference.Verify and follow up using a known trusted channel and retain communications.
Boards & risk governanceHow will legitimate transactions be affected during remediation?Affected service, processing time, complaints and corrections.Raise a service query or complaint so the operator can address individual harm.
Gaming & casino financial crimeWhere can a player seek help for financial harm or an account restriction?Player account, service record, available support and complaint arrangements.Seek support or correction through the right channel and obtain follow-up.
Crypto AML & AI automationWhich sensitive details reach a model, and could an error affect service?Permitted-use information, correction channel, service impact and follow-up records.Use the available channel to correct information or request human handling.

External partners

TopicQuestionEvidenceDecision
Stablecoins & tokenizationHow do the custodian, liquidity provider and payout bank acknowledge a handoff?Reconciliation items, shared reference IDs, service hours and failed-transfer handoffs.Identify the last holder of funds and follow agreed retry, return or escalation procedures.
Monitoring & financial intelligenceWhat facts allow a receiving team to act on a referral?Reference IDs, dates, links, provenance, permitted scope and acknowledgement.Validate links within authority and return permitted findings and outstanding gaps.
AI governance & RegTechCan the supplier reproduce a past output and explain data or model changes?Historical versions, feed updates, coverage limits, incident notices and data export capability.Disclose limits and repairs so the institution can decide permitted reliance.
FINTRAC examinationsCan the provider reproduce inputs and outputs for the incident period?Archives, delivery manifests, versions, incident notices and recovery tests.Support backfill and scope assessment, leaving validation of the fix to the institution.
Fraud disruption & law enforcementWhat can a telecom provider, recipient institution and police each verify?Signal confidence, permitted fields, retained records and lawful requests.Share the minimum permitted information and distinguish operational leads from formal evidence gathering.
Gaming & casino financial crimeWhat evidence can platform and payment partners lawfully provide for reconciliation?Data definitions, entitlements, references, delivery times and missing-record lists.Confirm delivery scope and unresolved gaps, with an auditable exception handoff.
Crypto AML & AI automationHow does a blockchain provider communicate uncertainty in indirect or cross-chain exposure?Label provenance, observation date, chain coverage, confidence and gaps.Deliver qualified intelligence and notify users of material attribution changes.

Third-party advisorSeek specialist interpretation or independent challenge when product scope, a model limitation or a remediation plan is uncertain.Give the advisor a defined question and an agreed evidence set. Record the advice, assumptions and any disagreement.Management names the decision owner and documents what it adopted and how it will test the result.
Regulator or supervisorUse the relevant authority’s channel for an examination request, applicable notification or scope question.Provide dated facts, the period and population affected, and the controls in place. Keep questions, responses and open items together.The institution assigns a response owner and verifies remediation. For a FINTRAC examination, link disputed facts to supporting records.
Financial crime task force or law-enforcement partnerCoordinate where a verified lead may support victim protection or disruption and a designated partner has a relevant mandate.Verify the recipient, purpose, authority and data that may be shared. Use the approved channel and record restrictions and follow-up.The fraud lead tracks intervention and customer outcome. The AML owner separately assesses reporting. Each partner acts within its authority.
